Diaspora Engagement & Advocacy

Building bridges between the diaspora and Kenya's development agenda

Advocacy & Policy Engagement

Working with Kenya's national and county governments to advance diaspora interests and investment opportunities.

Biashara Bridges plays a pivotal role in connecting the Kenyan diaspora with government initiatives, policy development, and investment programs that benefit both the diaspora and Kenya's development goals.

Our advocacy efforts have contributed to landmark achievements including the launch of Kenya's Diaspora Policy 2025 and strengthened partnerships with the State Department for Diaspora Affairs (SDDA).

Key Achievements

Milestones in diaspora engagement

Diaspora Policy 2025

Participated in the historic launch of Kenya's comprehensive Diaspora Policy, establishing frameworks for diaspora engagement and investment.

SDDA Partnership

Established strong working relationships with the State Department for Diaspora Affairs for ongoing collaboration.

Kenya-Ohio Visits

Facilitated high-level delegations between Kenya and Ohio, strengthening diplomatic and business relationships.

Engagement Highlights

SDDA Courtesy Visit

Delegation led by Mr. David Mutua met with SDDA officials to discuss diaspora investment opportunities and policy coordination.

Machakos-SEKEB Partnership

Facilitated partnership between Machakos County and SEKEB for enhanced diaspora engagement.

Trade Mission Coordination

Organized trade missions connecting diaspora investors with opportunities in Kenya.

Policy Advocacy

Active participation in shaping diaspora-friendly policies at national and county levels.
