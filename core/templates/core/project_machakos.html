Machakos County Partnership

Primary facilitator for U.S. diaspora-driven investment in Machakos County

Partnership Overview

Biashara Bridges LLC serves as the primary facilitator for U.S. diaspora-driven investment opportunities in Machakos County, Kenya.

In collaboration with Governor Wavinya Ndeti's administration, we have established a comprehensive framework for diaspora engagement and investment facilitation in the region.

Our partnership aims to mobilize diaspora capital, create sustainable development opportunities, and strengthen economic ties between Machakos County and the Kenyan diaspora in the United States.
