AggreBind Solutions Partnership

Sustainable construction solutions using locally available materials

Technology Partnership

Biashara Bridges is the exclusive local agent for AggreBind soil stabilization technology in Kenya.

AggreBind is a globally patented technology that enables sustainable construction using locally available materials. This partnership brings innovative, cost-effective solutions for infrastructure development in Kenya and East Africa.

Our role includes government engagement, project facilitation, compliance management, and investment mobilization to deploy this transformative technology across the region.
