About Us

Building bridges between Kenya, the U.S., and the world

Welcome to Biashara Bridges LLC

A U.S. and Kenya-registered firm founded by Kenyan diaspora professionals.

Biashara Bridges LLC operates at the intersection of diaspora networks, government institutions, and international businesses. We are committed to facilitating trusted, transparent, and transformative partnerships that unlock opportunities for Kenya, empower the diaspora, and strengthen global trade relations.

With strategic offices in Nairobi, Kenya and Cincinnati, Ohio, USA, we provide comprehensive services spanning:

G2G Partnerships
G2B Collaborations
B2B Trade
Diaspora Investments

Our Vision

To be the leading diaspora-driven bridge for international trade, investment, and development between Kenya, the U.S., and global partners.

Our Mission

To facilitate trusted, transparent, and transformative partnerships that unlock opportunities for Kenya, empower the diaspora, and strengthen global trade relations.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do
