Secure & Validated Documentation

Our Document Services streamline the often complex process of obtaining and validating official Kenyan documents for use in your host country, ensuring compliance and peace of mind.

1. Issuance of Certificates

We facilitate the swift and secure issuance of primary identity and vital records from the relevant Kenyan authorities, saving you time and travel expenses.

  • Birth and Death Certificates: expedited processing and delivery
  • Marriage Certificates: official copies for international use
  • Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) PIN Certificates
  • Good Conduct Certificates (Police Clearance)
Start Certificate Application

2. Document Legalization and Authentication

Ensure your documents are legally recognized overseas. We manage the notarization, commissioner of oaths stamping, and final diplomatic legalization required by international bodies.

  • Notarization and Commissioner of Oaths services
  • Apostille and Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MFA) endorsements
  • Embassy and High Commission legalization
  • Translation services for legal recognition (optional)
