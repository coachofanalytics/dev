Our Mission: Essential Support for the Global Kenyan

Diaspora County 48 (DC48K) is dedicated to being the essential link for all Kenyans living abroad. We offer comprehensive support—from **consular and crisis management** to **financial and community services**—ensuring you have the security, resources, and peace of mind to thrive globally while staying connected to home.
