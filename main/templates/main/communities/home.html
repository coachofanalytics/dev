Finding Your Home Away From Home

We believe a strong diaspora is a connected diaspora. Our community support initiatives are designed to foster belonging, facilitate knowledge sharing, and promote cultural exchange.

1. Forums and Networking

Connect with fellow members across the globe. Our private, moderated forums offer a space to share experiences, ask questions, and build professional and personal relationships within the Kenyan diaspora.

  • Verified Member Directory for professional networking
  • Regional and interest-based discussion forums
  • Mentorship programs connecting seasoned and new immigrants
  • Job and housing boards sourced within the community
Access Digital Forums

2. Community Events

We organize cultural, social, and professional events to bring the community together in person. Celebrate heritage, expand your network, and engage with community leaders.

  • Annual Diaspora Cultural Festivals and National Holidays
  • Professional development workshops and seminars
  • Regional meetups and family picnics
  • Volunteer and philanthropic opportunities
