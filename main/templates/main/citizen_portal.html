Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to process my application?

Processing times vary by service type. Birth certificates typically take 5-7 business days, while national ID replacements take 10-14 business days. You'll receive notifications as your application progresses.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, debit/credit cards, bank transfers, and cash payments at authorized agents. All transactions are secure and encrypted.

Can I track my application status?

Yes! You can track your application in real-time from the Applications page. Click the refresh icon to check for live updates, or view detailed information by clicking the eye icon.

How do I download my documents?

Once your application is approved, go to the Documents page and click the "Download" button next to your document. You can also view it online before downloading. Documents are available in PDF format.

What if my application is rejected?

If your application is rejected, you'll receive a detailed explanation via email and notification. You can resubmit with the required corrections or contact support for clarification.
