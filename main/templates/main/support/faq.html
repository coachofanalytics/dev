Help Center

Find answers to common questions or contact our team for help.

How do I become a volunteer?

To become a volunteer, complete the application form on our Volunteer page. We'll review your submission and get back to you within two weeks.

How can I donate?

You can donate via the Donate page, where we accept card and bank transfers. Receipts are emailed automatically.

How do I join events?

Events are listed on our Events page. Click an event to register — some events require approval.

Account or password help

Use the password reset link on the login page. If you still have issues, contact support and include your account email.
