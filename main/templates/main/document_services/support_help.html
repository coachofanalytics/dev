Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to process an application?

Processing times vary depending on the service type and document complexity:

  • • Birth Certificate: 3-5 business days
  • • National ID: 5-7 business days
  • • Marriage Certificate: 3-4 business days
  • • Death Certificate: 2-3 business days

You can track your application status in real-time in your dashboard.

What documents do I need to submit?

Required documents depend on the service. Generally, you'll need:

  • • Valid national identification
  • • Original or certified copy of the document you're requesting
  • • Proof of residence (utility bill, bank statement)
  • • Passport-sized photograph (for certain services)

Check the specific requirements for your service in the Document Requirements section.

Is my personal information secure?

Yes, we take security seriously. All your data is:

  • • Encrypted with industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols
  • • Stored in secure, audited data centers
  • • Protected by multi-factor authentication
  • • Never shared with third parties without your consent

We comply with all data protection and privacy regulations.

Can I cancel or refund my application?

Refund policies vary based on application status:

  • • Before Processing: Full refund within 24 hours
  • • During Processing: Partial refund (80% of payment)
  • • After Completion: No refund unless there's an error

Contact support for refund requests.
