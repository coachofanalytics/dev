Need Help?
Payment Not Appearing?

Check that M-Pesa is sending you payment prompts. Ensure you have a strong signal and that your phone number is registered with Safaricom.

How to Check Payment Status

You can check your M-Pesa statement by dialing *150*4*1# on your phone. Your transaction should appear there if it was processed.
