Requirements by User Type
For Staff Members
  • Active employment status
  • Minimum 3 months of consistent earnings
  • No outstanding loans
  • Guarantor: Current/active staff member
  • Guarantor Employment: 3+ months at CODA
  • Maximum loan: 2x monthly income
Staff members have access to emergency and development loans with competitive rates.
Guarantor Requirement: Must be a current/active staff member who has been employed for more than 3 months.
For KCC Members
  • Active KCC membership
  • Valid identification
  • Guarantor: KCC member with active membership
  • Tier System: Based on credit score (300-499, 500-699, 700+)
  • Premium loan terms available
  • Higher borrowing limits
KCC members enjoy exclusive loan products with the best rates.
Tier 1: 300-499 credit score, Tier 2: 500-699, Tier 3: 700+
For External Users
  • Valid identification
  • Proof of income
  • Guarantor: Always required
  • Collateral: Always required
  • Business plan (for business loans)
  • Additional documentation may be required
External users can access most loan products with proper documentation.
Requirements: Both guarantor and collateral are mandatory for external users.
Smart Collateral: Loans over $1,000 require smart collateral features:
  • Vehicle: GPS tracking enabled
  • Land: Verified title deed with government registry
  • Equipment: Serial number and condition report
