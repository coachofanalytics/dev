Join the millions of Kenyans living in the diaspora in different parts of the world. You can join as an ordinary member, as a leader or as an organisation. There are 47 counties in Kenya and ours is the 48th County of Kenya. Outlined below are the numerous benefits of becoming a member. Our motto is We are Stronger Together!

Unity and Collective Strength

  • We are Stronger Together – the strength of DC48K lies in its united membership.
  • Our membership is our power – it gives legitimacy and weight to the voice of the diaspora.
  • Validates and reinforces the diaspora’s demand for their constitutional, political, economic, and social rights as citizens of Kenya.

Networking and Professional Growth

  • DC48K serves as a dynamic County for connecting with fellow Kenyans and experts across various sectors.
  • Offers opportunities for professional networking, collaboration, and relationship-building.
  • Hosts events such as the Diaspora Homeland Summit (DAHOS), ideal for exchanging ideas and forging partnerships.
  • Supports members in expanding their networks and growing professionally.
  • Helps members stay connected to their cultural and national identity.

Knowledge Sharing and Economic Empowerment

  • Plays a key role in disseminating relevant knowledge about developments in Kenya.
  • Provides access to expert insights and information on career growth and cultural events.
  • Supports economic empowerment by identifying investment opportunities in: Real Estate, Infrastucture, Green bonds
  • Facilitates secure and transparent investments through partnerships with:Capital Markets Authority (CMA)
  • Enhances social welfare by providing a reliable social networking County that strengthens community ties.
  • Fosters a strong sense of belonging and offers a “home away from home".

Advocacy, Social Support, and Welfare Services

  • Committed to advocacy and representation of Kenyans living abroad.
  • Champions the rights and welfare of the diaspora community.
  • Actively engages in policy advocacy and governance processes in Kenya.
  • Ensures diaspora voices are heard in national development efforts
  • Provides accessible and affordable insurance coverage, including:
    • Emergency medical and hospital expenses
    • Evacuation in times of distress or deportation
    • Repatriation of mortal remains or support for burial abroad
  • Offers comprehensive social support services to its members.
  • Reinforces the mission to protect, empower, and unify Kenyans abroad.
