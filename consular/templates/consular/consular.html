How We Help You Thrive Abroad

Navigating international bureaucracy can be tough. Our Consular Assistance services ensure that whether you need a travel document, legal advice, or urgent help, you have a clear, reliable channel back to Kenyan government support.

1. Information and Updates

Stay informed on critical changes to Kenyan policies, travel advisories, and diaspora-specific news. We provide real-time alerts and verified government communications directly to our members, preventing misinformation and delays.

  • Passport and Visa Renewals: Direct guidance and forms
  • Travel Advisories: Real-time safety updates by country
  • Government Policy Changes: Summaries of diaspora relevance
  • Emergency Contact Directory: Vetted consular numbers worldwide
View Official Forms

2. Legal and Immigration Guidance

Access reliable, vetted legal resources for common issues faced by the diaspora, including property disputes, dual citizenship applications, and immigration challenges in host countries. We connect you with trusted professionals.

  • Legal Referrals: Access to diaspora-friendly lawyers
  • Property and Land Dispute Mediation: Guidance on Kenyan law
  • Dual Citizenship Application Support: Step-by-step process assistance
  • Wills and Estate Planning: Connecting with notaries and legal experts
