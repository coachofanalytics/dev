About us

Biashara Bridges LLC is a U.S. and Kenya registered company, founded by members of the Kenyan Diaspora living in the United States. With offices in Nairobi and the U.S., we specialize in facilitating:

  • Government-to-Government (G2G) partnerships
  • Government-to-Business (G2B) collaborations
  • Business-to-Business (B2B) trade and investments

We mobilize diaspora capital, connect investors with vetted opportunities, and create structured investment vehicles that deliver sustainable development.

What We Do

Biashara Bridges serves as a trusted facilitator for:

  • Diaspora-Driven Investments mobilizing capital, expertise, and networks from the U.S.-based Kenyan diaspora.
  • International Trade & B2B Partnerships connecting companies and institutions across borders.
  • Government Engagement aligning projects with national and county priorities to ensure impact and sustainability.
  • Advisory & Facilitation offering tailored services that de-risk and unlock investment opportunities.
