# Pricing Tier Restructure Proposal

## Current vs Proposed Structure

### Current Problem:
- Consultative ($420) doesn't justify premium vs Balanced ($249)
- Elite ($399) is higher than Consultative but less service
- Confusion about what you get for what you pay

---

## Proposed 4-Tier Structure

### Concept: Progressive Service Levels
**Progression:** Signal-only → Automation → Automation + Oversight → **Full Automation + Full Management**

---

## Detailed Feature Comparison

### Tier 1: Starter ($99/mo)
**Service Model:** Signal-Only (Self-Service)
- ✅ Email alerts with signals
- ✅ Basic portfolio suggestions
- ❌ No automation
- ✅ You execute & manage all positions
- ✅ Email support only
- **Best For:** Beginners learning options, low capital, want signals only

---

### Tier 2: Balanced ($249/mo)
**Service Model:** Automated Signals (Self-Service)
- ✅ Auto-ranked portfolio sleeves delivered
- ✅ SMS/WhatsApp alerts when signals ready
- ✅ Automated refresh (2x daily feed)
- ✅ You execute & manage all positions
- ✅ Standard support
- **Best For:** Active traders who want automation but manage themselves

---

### Tier 3: Elite ($399/mo)
**Service Model:** Automation + CODA Oversight
- ✅ Premium signals + full automation
- ✅ CODA monitors your positions (not managing)
- ✅ Risk alerts & recommendations
- ✅ You execute, CODA watches & advises
- ✅ Priority support + weekly reviews
- ✅ Auto-entry capability (when broker API ready)
- **Best For:** Serious traders who want automation + oversight

---

### Tier 4: Consultative ($420/mo) ⭐ **PREMIUM TIER**
**Service Model:** Full Automation + Full Management
- ✅ **CODA sets positions** (fully automated)
- ✅ **CODA executes positions** (when broker API ready)
- ✅ **CODA manages all positions** (position lifecycle)
- ✅ **CODA monitors risk** (full risk management)
- ✅ Completely hands-off for client
- ✅ Dedicated account manager
- ✅ Premium WhatsApp alerts
- ✅ Weekly performance reports
- ❌ **NO coaching sessions** (pure managed trading)
- **Best For:** Busy professionals, institutions, hands-off investors

## Why This Makes Sense

### Clear Value Proposition:

| If you want... | Choose this tier |
|----------------|------------------|
| Just signals, manage yourself, lowest cost | **Starter** ($99/mo) |
| Automation but manage yourself, best value | **Balanced** ($249/mo) |
| Automation + oversight, premium signals | **Elite** ($399/mo) |
| **Hands-off, CODA does everything** | **Consultative** ($420/mo) ⭐ |

### Why Consultative ($420) is Worth It:

**You pay $420/mo for:**
- ✅ CODA selects the best positions
- ✅ CODA executes trades (when API ready)
- ✅ CODA manages position lifecycle
- ✅ CODA monitors risk 24/7
- ✅ Dedicated account manager
- ✅ Weekly performance reports
- ✅ Premium support

**vs. Elite ($399) where:**
- ✅ CODA provides signals & automation
- ✅ CODA monitors (but you execute/manage)
- ❌ You still execute trades yourself
- ❌ You still manage positions yourself
- ✅ Priority support (but not dedicated manager)

**The $21/mo difference = Full management service**
