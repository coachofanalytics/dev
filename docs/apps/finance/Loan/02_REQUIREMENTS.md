## 📜 BUSINESS RULES

### BR-001: Eligibility Criteria

**Staff Loans:**
- Minimum 3 months employment
- No outstanding defaults
- Maximum 3x monthly salary

**KCC Loans:**
- Active KCC membership
- Minimum 6 months membership
- No outstanding KCC defaults
