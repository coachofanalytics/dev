# Loan System - Analysis

**Last Updated:** October 22, 2025

---

## 🎯 PROBLEM STATEMENT

CODA needs an integrated loan management system for staff loans and KCC (Kenya Commercial Credit) partnership integration.

---

## 👥 USER PAIN POINTS

- Staff need access to emergency loans
- KCC members need streamlined loan application
- Manual eligibility checking time-consuming
- No automated payment tracking

---

## 🎯 BUSINESS GOALS

1. **Staff Welfare:** Provide emergency loan facility
2. **KCC Integration:** Support partner organization loans
3. **Automation:** Reduce manual eligibility checking
4. **Compliance:** Full audit trail for all loans

---
