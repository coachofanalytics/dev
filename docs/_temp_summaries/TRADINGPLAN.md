Trading Strategy & Plan for $30,000 Capital
Executive Summary
This document outlines a comprehensive trading plan for a starting capital of $30,000, aimed at achieving a consistent monthly return using a conservative yet profitable options trading strategy. The plan incorporates both large-cap and small-cap stocks, utilizing a mix of momentum, reversal, and consolidation strategies, leveraging options flow data and tools such as Unusual Whales and Options Play to enhance decision-making.
Trading Strategy Plan
The strategy is designed to provide a balance between **high-risk/high-reward small-cap trades** and **low-risk/high-probability large-cap trades**, using options spreads, Iron Condors, and straddle strategies to minimize risk while maximizing profit potential.
Detailed Breakdown
Large-Cap Setup (20-30% Jumps)
For large-cap stocks, the focus will be on trades with **5-15% price moves** and continuation momentum. Using tools like **RSI**, **Bollinger Bands**, and **MACD**, trades will be executed primarily in **call spreads**, with conservative stop-loss and take-profit levels. Risk per trade is capped at 2% of the total capital, ensuring that no more than $600 is risked on any single trade.
Small-Cap Setup (30-40% Jumps)
Small-cap stocks, typically more volatile, will be used for **higher-risk trades** involving **call spreads** and **short puts**. The strategy focuses on **small-cap momentum**, where large jumps (30-40%) indicate strong institutional backing. Risk per small-cap trade will be capped at **3% of total capital**, which is $900, with targeted monthly profits of $1,125 – $1,875.
Monthly Trading Target
The monthly return target is set at **8% - 12%**, with **$30,000 capital** leading to a projected monthly profit of **$2,550 - $3,300**. This is achieved through a combination of large-cap and small-cap options trades, along with strategic volatility plays.
Risk Management
To maintain consistency and avoid significant losses, risk management will be strictly adhered to. Each trade will risk no more than **2-3% of total capital**, with a max drawdown rule set at 5% per week. The **stopa-loss and take-profit strategy** is dynamic and uses ATR (Average True Range) for adjusting position size and exit points.
Data Sources & Tools
The trading strategy is enhanced by integrating data from **Unusual Whales** and **Options Play** to track **unusual options activity** and **high-probability trades**. These platforms help filter trades based on **institutional flow**, **smart money** movements, and **high-volume options activity**. The goal is to increase **trade accuracy** by entering positions backed by significant institutional activity.
Impact of Capital Scaling
As capital increases from **$30,000 to $40,000 or $50,000**, the ability to deploy larger positions increases, allowing for **greater diversification** and the potential for **higher returns**. However, the strategy will remain conservative, with risk per trade adjusted accordingly.
BRD and FRD for Automation
The **Business Requirements Document (BRD)** and **Functional Requirements Document (FRD)** outline the automation of the trading strategy using **Python Django**. The goal is to automate **trade execution, data collection, and risk management**, with integration of tools like **Options Play** and **Unusual Whales** to drive signal generation.
